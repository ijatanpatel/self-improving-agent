# Automations

## How automations work
An automation is a rule of the form "When this happens, do that". For example: when a task's status changes to Done, move it to the Archive list. Create rules from the lightning icon in the project header.

## Triggers and actions
Triggers include a status change, a due date approaching, a task being assigned and a form being submitted. Actions include assigning someone, moving a task, setting a field and posting a message to Slack.

## Monthly run limits
Each time a rule fires, it counts as one run. Workspaces get 50 runs per month on Free, 1,000 on Team, 10,000 on Business and unlimited runs on Enterprise. The counter resets on your billing date.

## When you hit the limit
Admins get an email when the workspace has used 80% and 100% of its runs. Once the limit is reached, automations pause until the counter resets or the workspace upgrades. Rules are not deleted.
