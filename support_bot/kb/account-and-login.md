# Account and login

## Resetting your password
Click "Forgot password" on the login page and enter your email address. We send a reset link that is valid for 60 minutes. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder and make sure you used the address your account was created with.

## Signing in with Google or Microsoft
You can sign in with a Google or Microsoft account that uses the same email address as your Orbitask account. If your workspace enforces SAML single sign-on, you must use your company's sign-in page instead.

## Locked accounts
After 10 failed login attempts in a row, the account is locked for 30 minutes to protect it. Wait 30 minutes and try again, or reset your password to unlock it right away.

## Changing your email address
Go to Profile > Account and enter the new address. We send a confirmation link to the new address, and the change only takes effect after you click it.

## Deleting your account
Go to Profile > Account > Delete account. If you are the Owner of a workspace, you must transfer ownership to another Admin first. Deleted accounts can be restored by support for 30 days; after that, the account and its personal data are permanently removed.
