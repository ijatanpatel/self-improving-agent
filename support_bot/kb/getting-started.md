# Getting started with Orbitask

## Creating your workspace
Sign up at app.orbitask.example/signup with your work email, or continue with Google or Microsoft. Signing up creates a workspace, and you become its Owner. A workspace holds all of your team's projects, members and settings. You can belong to several workspaces and switch between them from the workspace menu in the top-left corner.

## Inviting teammates
Open Settings > Members and click Invite. You can invite people by email or share an invite link. Invite links expire after 7 days and can be turned off at any time. Invited people join as Members by default; an Admin can change their role later.

## Your first project
Click New project and pick a template: Kanban board, Sprint planning, Content calendar or Blank. Templates only set up lists and fields, so you can change everything afterwards. Press the ? key anywhere in the app to see all keyboard shortcuts.

## Supported browsers
Orbitask works on the latest two versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. Internet Explorer is not supported. For the best experience on phones, use the mobile apps instead of a mobile browser.
