# Mobile apps and offline mode

## Supported devices
The Orbitask apps run on iPhone and iPad with iOS 16 or later and on Android 10 or later. Download them from the App Store or Google Play and sign in with the same account you use on the web.

## Working offline
In the mobile apps you can view and edit tasks without an internet connection. Your changes are saved on the device and synced automatically when you are back online.

## Sync conflicts
If someone else changed the same field while you were offline, the most recent change wins. The earlier change is not lost: it is kept in the task's history, where you can restore it.

## Attachments offline
Attachments are only available offline if you opened them while you were online. Uploading new attachments needs a connection; uploads you start offline begin as soon as you reconnect.
