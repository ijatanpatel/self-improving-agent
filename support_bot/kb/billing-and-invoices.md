# Billing and invoices

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express credit or debit cards, and PayPal. Business and Enterprise customers on annual plans can also pay by invoice through bank transfer, with payment due within 30 days. Prices are in US dollars; customers with a billing address in the EU are billed in euros.

## When you are charged
Your billing date is the day you first upgraded. If you add members in the middle of a billing period, you are charged a prorated amount for the rest of that period. If you remove members, the unused time is added as a credit to your next invoice; it is not refunded to your card.

## Downloading invoices
Owners and Admins can download every invoice as a PDF from Settings > Billing > Invoices. Invoices are also emailed to the billing contact on the day of each charge. To change the billing contact, edit the email address in Settings > Billing.

## Tax IDs (VAT and GST)
Add your VAT or GST number in Settings > Billing > Tax ID. The tax ID appears on invoices issued after you add it; we cannot change invoices that were already issued.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, we retry it 3 times over 10 days and email the Owner each time. If all retries fail, the workspace moves to read-only mode until the payment details are updated. No data is deleted.
