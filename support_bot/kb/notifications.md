# Notifications

## Where notifications appear
You get notifications in the in-app Inbox, by email and as push notifications in the mobile apps. You choose which channels to use in Profile > Notifications.

## Daily email digest
Instead of one email per update, you can receive a daily digest. It is sent at 8:00 in the morning in the timezone set on your profile and lists everything that happened in the last 24 hours.

## Quiet hours
Quiet hours stop push notifications during the hours you choose, for example at night. Notifications still collect in your Inbox and are delivered when quiet hours end.

## Mentions and per-project settings
When someone @mentions you, you are always notified unless you have muted the project. Each project can be set to "All activity", "Only mentions" or "Off" from the bell icon in the project header.
