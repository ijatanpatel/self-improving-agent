# Google Calendar integration

## What syncs
Tasks that are assigned to you and have a due date appear in a separate "Orbitask" calendar in your Google Calendar. The sync is two-way: if you move an event in Google Calendar, the task's due date changes in Orbitask too. Tasks without a due date, and tasks assigned to other people, do not sync.

## Turning it on
Go to Profile > Integrations > Google Calendar and sign in with your Google account. The Google Calendar integration is available on the Team plan and above.

## How fast changes appear
Changes made in Orbitask usually reach Google Calendar within a minute. Changes made in Google Calendar are picked up every 15 minutes, because that is how often Google lets us check for updates.

## Other calendars
Orbitask can also publish a read-only iCal feed of your tasks from Profile > Integrations > iCal feed. You can subscribe to this feed from Apple Calendar or any calendar app that supports iCal links. The iCal feed is one-way only.
