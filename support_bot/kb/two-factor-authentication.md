# Two-factor authentication (2FA)

## Turning on 2FA
Go to Profile > Security > Two-factor authentication and scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator, Authy or 1Password. Enter the 6-digit code to finish. Orbitask does not support SMS codes for 2FA.

## Backup codes
When you turn on 2FA, we show 10 one-time backup codes. Store them somewhere safe. Each code works once, and you can generate a new set at any time, which cancels the old codes.

## If you lose your phone
Sign in with one of your backup codes and then set up 2FA again on your new device. If you have no backup codes, contact support. We will turn off 2FA after verifying your identity, which can take up to 3 business days.

## Requiring 2FA for everyone
On the Business and Enterprise plans, Admins can require 2FA for all members in Settings > Security. Members who have not set up 2FA are asked to do it the next time they sign in and cannot open the workspace until they do.
