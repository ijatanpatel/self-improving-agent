# Security and privacy

## Encryption
Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or newer. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

## Where data is hosted
By default, data is hosted in the United States. Enterprise customers can choose to host their workspace in the European Union instead. Data residency in other regions is not currently offered.

## Compliance
Orbitask has a SOC 2 Type II report, which Business and Enterprise customers can request under an NDA. A data processing agreement (DPA) for GDPR is available on all paid plans from Settings > Billing > Legal.

## Retention and backups
Deleted items stay in Trash for 30 days before they are permanently removed. Backups are kept for 35 days and are only used for disaster recovery, not to restore individual items. We never sell customer data.
