# Workspace roles and members

## Roles
Every workspace has exactly one Owner, who manages billing and can delete the workspace. Admins manage members, workspace settings and integrations. Members create and work on projects and tasks. Guests only see the projects they are invited to and cannot see the member list.

## Guests
Guests are free. On the Team plan, each paid member can bring up to 2 guests; on Business, up to 5. Guests cannot create projects or install integrations. The Free plan does not include guests.

## Removing a member
Admins can remove a member from Settings > Members. The person's tasks, comments and files stay in the workspace. Tasks assigned to them stay assigned and are marked "former member" so you can reassign them.

## Transferring ownership
The Owner can transfer ownership from Settings > Workspace > Transfer ownership. The new Owner must already be an Admin. After the transfer, the previous Owner becomes an Admin.
