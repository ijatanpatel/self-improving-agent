# Importing and exporting data

## Importing
You can import tasks from a CSV file, from a Trello board export (JSON) or directly from Asana by connecting your Asana account. Go to New project > Import and follow the steps. Imported tasks keep their names, descriptions, due dates, assignees (matched by email) and comments.

## Exporting a project
Open the project menu and choose Export to download the project as CSV or JSON. Exports include tasks, subtasks, custom fields and comments. Attachments are not included in a single-project export.

## Full workspace export
On Business and Enterprise, Admins can export the whole workspace from Settings > Workspace > Export. We email a download link when the export is ready; the link is valid for 7 days. Full exports include attachments in a ZIP file.
