# Contacting support

## Help center and community
Everyone can use the help center and the community forum at community.orbitask.example. Free plan workspaces are supported through these two channels only.

## Email support
Paid plans can email support@orbitask.example. Our first-response targets are 24 business hours on Team, 4 business hours on Business, and 1 hour for urgent issues on Enterprise, around the clock.

## Live chat
Business and Enterprise customers can use live chat from the ? menu in the app, Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 18:00 Central European Time.

## Phone support
We only offer phone support to Enterprise customers, through their dedicated customer success manager.

## Service status
Check status.orbitask.example to see whether there is an ongoing incident before contacting support. You can subscribe to incident updates by email on that page.
