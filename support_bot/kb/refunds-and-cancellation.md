# Refunds and cancellation

## Cancelling your subscription
The workspace Owner can cancel at any time from Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription. After you cancel, the workspace keeps its paid features until the end of the current billing period and then moves to the Free plan.

## Refunds on monthly plans
Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months. When you cancel, you keep access until the end of the month you have paid for.

## Refunds on annual plans
Annual plans can be fully refunded if you cancel within 14 days of the purchase or of an automatic renewal. After 14 days we do not give refunds, but you can turn off automatic renewal so you are not charged again.

## Duplicate or unexpected charges
If you see a duplicate charge, contact support with the invoice numbers. Once we confirm the duplicate, the refund is sent to the original payment method and usually arrives within 5 to 7 business days.

## What happens to your data after a downgrade
When a paid workspace moves to the Free plan, nothing is deleted. Projects beyond the Free limit of 3 become read-only archives, and features such as Timeline view and automations above 50 runs stop working until you upgrade again.
