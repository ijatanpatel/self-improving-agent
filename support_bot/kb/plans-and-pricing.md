# Plans and pricing

## Free plan
The Free plan costs nothing and has no time limit. It includes up to 5 members, 3 active projects, 1 GB of storage per workspace, a 25 MB maximum file size and 50 automation runs per month. Support on the Free plan is through the help center and the community forum.

## Team plan
The Team plan costs $8 per member per month when billed annually, or $10 per member per month when billed monthly. It adds unlimited projects, Timeline view, 100 GB of storage per workspace, a 250 MB maximum file size, 1,000 automation runs per month, API access and email support. Each paid member on Team can bring up to 2 guests for free.

## Business plan
The Business plan costs $16 per member per month billed annually, or $19 billed monthly. It includes everything in Team plus SAML single sign-on, enforced two-factor authentication, an audit log kept for 1 year, task dependencies, password-protected share links, 500 GB of storage, a 1 GB maximum file size, 10,000 automation runs per month and priority support. Each paid member can bring up to 5 guests.

## Enterprise plan
Enterprise pricing is custom and depends on the number of seats and the contract. It adds SCIM user provisioning, a choice of US or EU data hosting, unlimited automation runs, a dedicated customer success manager and 24/7 support for urgent issues. Contact sales from the pricing page to get a quote.

## Changing plans
Owners can upgrade or downgrade from Settings > Billing. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.
